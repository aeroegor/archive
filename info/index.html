Egor Sevastyanov

I am a composer, sound artist and filmmaker. I call my approach documentary music. Real sounds, radio and voices become the material of a piece, and reality becomes its co-author.

I improvise live on modular synthesizers, record in the field and from the radio, compose with speech and make films. Since 2025 I have been running PeaceTime Station, a series of long live transmissions. More than a hundred sets have been played so far.

I have made dozens of documentary films, feature-length and short, as director, cinematographer, sound recordist and editor. I have taught sound design at the New Cinema School.
