The Most Beautiful Thing I've Ever Seen

2024

Documentary sound work / speech composition

Duration: 11 min
Full listening recommended.

This recording was never meant to become a work. It happened on the side, while something else was being recorded. But already then I could feel the form it wanted to take.

The piece is made only of voices. Words return again and again, never quite the same.

Everything rests on a single clap. It holds the whole piece together, and everything else unfolds around it.

Recorded and composed:
